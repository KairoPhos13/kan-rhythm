Share your levels with everyone who plays kan!, and choose your instrument where you choose the level.

Website: https://kan.kairophos.link

## New

- **Submit your levels from kan!forge.** Sign in, open your level in the editor and choose *File > Submit level...*. The level is published under your name and shows on your profile. Post it as *Work in progress* while you are still shaping it, or as *Pending* when it is finished and ready to be reviewed for ranking. Ranked levels appear in the main listing and get a leaderboard. Before you submit, check that you may share both the music and every audio file in your level: the [level creator's handbook](https://github.com/KairoPhos13/kan-rhythm/blob/main/game/docs/level-creation-guide.md) explains what you may use.
- **Your instrument, under the level.** In song select, right under the level's title and stars, kan! shows your instrument, the notes it plays and how the selected level fits it. Click it to change them; the list of levels follows right away. The panel at the right of the loading screen now holds only the notation and the microphone.
- **The level creator's handbook:** how to turn a song into a level, tab by tab, with a worked example. It is a first draft, and your questions will make it better.

## Changed

- Splitting a song into its parts now downloads a separation model with a clear open licence (MIT). It is larger than before (about 350 MB, downloaded once, the first time you split a song).
- Every download now carries the licence and the full list of third-party components (`LICENCE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`) next to the game.
- Your profile shows when you last played.

## Install

As in 0.1.0: download the zip for your system below, unzip it and open kan!. If you already have 0.1.0, replace its folder with the new one; your levels, scores and settings stay where they are.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `kan-win-x64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon | `kan-osx-arm64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Intel | `kan-osx-x64.zip` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `kan-linux-x64.zip` |

kan! is not signed yet. Windows: *More info*, then *Run anyway*. macOS: Control-click kan! and choose *Open*.
