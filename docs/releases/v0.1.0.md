kan! is a free, calm rhythm game you play on a real instrument. It listens through your microphone and follows the notes you play. This is the first public version: early, made for calm practice, and open to everyone who wants to help it grow.

Website: https://kan.kairophos.link

## What you can do

- **Play** levels on your instrument: notes travel on lanes by pitch, and kan! checks the pitch and the timing as you play. Forgiving by default; strict judging only if you choose it.
- **Practise** with mods: *Wait for me* (the level waits for each note), *Practice* (loop a part, slow it down), *Half time*, *Easy*, and challenges such as *By heart* and *Hard rock*.
- **Make levels** in kan!forge: bring a song from your computer, let it write the notes, correct them in a piano roll, and test the level before you share it.
- **Split a song into its parts** on your computer: the companion plays under you while you play; the reference is the full performance to learn from. The first separation downloads the separation model once.
- **An account, if you want one**: level downloads and leaderboards on kan!'s own server. Create it on the website or in the game. Playing and making levels never need it.

## Instruments

Tested on the ocarina (developed on a 12-hole alto C ocarina) and on instruments that play one note at a time. Other instruments and chords will come as the community tests them: if you play something else, your feedback shapes what comes next.

## Install

Download the zip for your system below, unzip it and open kan!. You need a microphone; the one in a laptop works.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `kan-win-x64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon | `kan-osx-arm64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Intel | `kan-osx-x64.zip` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `kan-linux-x64.zip` |

kan! is not signed yet, so your system warns you the first time:

- **Windows:** "Windows protected your PC". Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** "cannot check the developer". Control-click kan! and choose *Open*.

## Good to know

- The microphone is analysed on your computer while you play. Nothing it hears is recorded or sent.
- kan! sends no email, so there is no password reset yet. Keep your password somewhere safe; if you forget it, see https://kan.kairophos.link/#password.
- The level catalogue is just starting: the first free levels arrive soon.
- Not there yet: chat, multiplayer and spectating. The intro is silent until kan! has its own music.
- The interface is in English. Other languages still show some text from osu!.
- Windows and macOS builds are new and still need testing on real machines. Please tell us what you find: https://github.com/KairoPhos13/kan-rhythm/issues
