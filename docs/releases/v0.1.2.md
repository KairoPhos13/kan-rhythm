Five levels to play as soon as kan! opens, no account needed.

Website: https://kan.kairophos.link

## New

- **The starter pack.** The first time kan! runs, five levels are already in song select, made for kan! from music that belongs to everyone:
  - *Sakura Sakura* (Japan)
  - *Kojo no Tsuki*, Rentaro Taki (Japan, 1901)
  - *Mo Li Hua*, Jasmine Flower (China, as John Barrow wrote it down in 1804)
  - *Xicochi* (Mexico, after Gaspar Fernández, c. 1601), a new setting of ours
  - *Amazing Grace* (hymn, tune NEW BRITAIN)

  They are easy to moderate and sit in the range of a 12-hole alto C ocarina, and kan! fits them to whatever you play. Each level opens with one bar of soft pulses so you hear the tempo before the first note. Each level's credits list its sources and licences, and all the levels' files are free to share (CC0).

## Install

As before: download the zip for your system below, unzip it and open kan!. If you already have kan!, replace its folder with the new one; your levels, scores and settings stay where they are, and the five new levels appear the next time you open it.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `kan-win-x64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon | `kan-osx-arm64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Intel | `kan-osx-x64.zip` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `kan-linux-x64.zip` |

kan! is not signed yet. Windows: *More info*, then *Run anyway*. macOS: Control-click kan! and choose *Open*.
