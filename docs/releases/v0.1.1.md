Five levels to play as soon as kan! opens, a short guide to set up your instrument, and a way to share your own levels with everyone who plays kan!.

Website: https://kan.kairophos.link

## New

- **The starter pack.** The first time kan! runs, five levels are already in song select, made for kan! from music that belongs to everyone:
  - *Sakura Sakura* (Japan)
  - *Kojo no Tsuki*, Rentaro Taki (Japan, 1901)
  - *Mo Li Hua*, Jasmine Flower (China, as John Barrow wrote it down in 1804)
  - *Xicochi* (Mexico, after Gaspar Fernández, c. 1601), a new setting of ours
  - *Amazing Grace* (hymn, tune NEW BRITAIN)

  They are easy to moderate and sit in the range of a 12-hole alto C ocarina, and kan! fits them to whatever you play. Each level opens with one bar of soft pulses so you hear the tempo before the first note. Each level's credits list its sources and licences, and all the levels' files are free to share (CC0).
- **A first-run guide.** On a fresh install kan! walks you through choosing your instrument, your microphone and a short calibration, then suggests a first level. Every step can be skipped, and the guide runs again from *Settings > Rulesets > kan!*.
- **Calibration.** Three quiet measurements: your microphone's delay, the sound of your room, and your instrument's tuning. A slightly flat or sharp instrument is never punished: kan! learns to follow it.
- **Colours and text for everyone.** Note colours for the common kinds of colour blindness, and a text size setting for notes and labels.
- **Submit your levels from kan!forge.** Sign in, open your level in the editor and choose *File > Submit level...*. The level is published under your name and shows on your profile. Post it as *Work in progress* while you are still shaping it, or as *Pending* when it is finished and ready to be reviewed for ranking. Ranked levels appear in the main listing and get a leaderboard. Before you submit, check that you may share both the music and every audio file in your level: the [level creator's handbook](https://github.com/KairoPhos13/kan-rhythm/blob/main/game/docs/level-creation-guide.md) explains what you may use.
- **Your instrument, under the level.** In song select, right under the level's title and stars, kan! shows your instrument, the notes it plays and how the selected level fits it. Click it to change them; the list of levels follows right away. The panel at the right of the loading screen now holds only the notation and the microphone.
- **The level creator's handbook:** how to turn a song into a level, tab by tab, with a worked example. It is a first draft, and your questions will make it better.

## Changed

- Splitting a song into its parts now downloads a separation model with a clear open licence (MIT). It is larger than before (about 350 MB, downloaded once, the first time you split a song).
- Every download now carries the licence, the full list of third-party components (`LICENCE`, `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`) and the complete licence texts (`licenses/`) next to the game.
- Your profile shows when you last played.

## Fixed

- Levels now show their real length and difficulty in song select (some read 00:00 and 0 stars).
- One wrong note played just before a chord now counts once, not once per note of the chord.
- Rewinding a replay no longer marks the rewound notes as not heard.
- A score with impossible numbers is repaired when it opens, instead of making the level impossible to save.
- The game no longer needs a recent processor: kan! runs on older computers too.
- The server got faster and safer: large or broken uploads are refused early, and a busy player can no longer lock others out of signing in.

## Install

As in 0.1.0: download the zip for your system below, unzip it and open kan!. If you already have 0.1.0, replace its folder with the new one; your levels, scores and settings stay where they are, and the five new levels appear the next time you open it.

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit | `kan-win-x64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon | `kan-osx-arm64.zip` |
| macOS 13 or newer, Intel | `kan-osx-x64.zip` |
| Linux, 64-bit | `kan-linux-x64.zip` |

kan! is not signed yet. Windows: *More info*, then *Run anyway*. macOS: Control-click kan! and choose *Open*.
